  Two bugs found on the way, both fixed: a scratch spilled across a jsr slot restored over the borrowed register
  (Release idle in a third of the time with the registry hashing gone), and the re-park word at a helper's return

  of the boot (first profiles were slower than before the change); it now decodes once.
